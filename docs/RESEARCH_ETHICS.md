# Research Ethics and Scope

This project investigates the behavior of general-purpose language models in **simulated mental-health-related conversations**, with input from clinical collaborators.

## Intended use

The material in this repository documents an academic research effort. It is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, crisis intervention, or clinical decision-making.

## Clinical interpretation

Model behavior must be assessed with appropriate caution. A response that appears empathetic or recognizes a cue in a simulated exchange does not establish safety, diagnostic accuracy, therapeutic competence, or suitability for patient care.

## Responsible disclosure

Detailed study materials and outcomes are withheld while the work remains on progress. Before making any future artifacts public, the research team will review them for privacy, permissions, scientific integrity, and potential risks of misinterpretation or misuse.

## Emergency situations

If someone may be in immediate danger, they should contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional rather than rely on an AI model or this repository.
